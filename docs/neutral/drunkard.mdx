# Drunk-ard

The Drunk-ard is a neutral player who does not currently know their actual role, but they are guaranteed a Village-aligned role.

The Drunk-ard is a seldom used role and most often used to help a Moderator rebalance large setups.

## Role Type

- The Drunk-ard is seen as a Neutral Party by the Seer.
- The Drunk-ard is not seen as a user of witchcraft.
- The Drunk-ard does not have a night action so will not be seen visiting by the Harlot/Stalker/Familiar.
- The Drunk-ard is not seen as a killer by the Adjudicator.

## Notes

- Sobering up can happen at any time, including in the middle of the day.
- Until such time as the Drunk-ard sobers up they count as a member of the Village when evaluating the win-conditions for the other factions.
- There is no fixed time as to when the Drunk-ard sobers up, but when they do they fully embrace their new role.
- A Drunk-ard player might also be given items when they sober up.

## Tips

- As there is no fixed-time for when the Drunk-ard will sober up, nor any guarantee as to which role they sober up into.
